# Go-to-market playbooks for creator trading, copy-trading and trading-bot features (2023–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-01. Most figures are company-reported or come from crypto media and review sites. Each claim is tagged with its date and whether it is self-reported.

## Creator acquisition: how platforms recruited and paid lead traders and KOLs, and cold-start tactics

### Takeaway
Every major platform pays creators through a mix of (a) a default **10% profit share** (Binance, Hyperliquid vaults, Bitget's lower tiers), (b) higher-tier upgrades for top performers (up to 20–30%), (c) **AUM-based stipends** (eToro), and (d) recurring **lead-trader tournaments** where half the prize goes to the leader and half to their copiers (Bybit). Cold-start tactics include funded starter accounts, promotions open only to leaders with few copiers, and "exposure" (placement on the leaderboard or homepage) as a reward.

### Cited Findings
**Binance (official support pages, 2024–2025)**
- Lead traders get a 10% profit share from copiers plus a 10% commission on copiers' trading fees, on both futures and spot. Elite lead traders can go up to 30% profit share. — [Binance Spot Copy Trading Elite Trader Program Guide](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/2fc0125418c94bfcbefc8f9b4fe5aa72); [Binance Spot Copy Trading Guide](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/abc27f5949cf46d9801d31b5e33d0b38)
- Binance upgraded its "Lead Trader Growth Plan" on 2025-03-27. It ran a spot copy-trading campaign from 2025-04-10 to 2025-05-08 with a 20,000 USDC pool, paying 1 USDC for a mock portfolio that reached +5% PnL (a cheap way to get people to try it with no risk). Limits are 1 lead account and 10 copy accounts per user, and a spot lead portfolio starts with a cap of 300 copiers. — [Binance announcement](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/announcement/detail/2431be3394b348dfa90d4e3658335ac5)

**Bitget Elite Trader program (help center, 2023–2026)**
- Leaders must pass KYC and apply. Profit share uses a **high-water mark**, so leaders earn only on new copier profits. It settles daily, and nothing is paid on losing orders. Older tier caps: Bronze/Silver 10%, Gold 12%, Platinum 14%, Extraordinary 15%, Legend 20%. — [Bitget support](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603838677)
- The Elite Trader Center shows leaders their copier count, **copier retention rate**, AUM and time-weighted return. In October 2025 Bitget added futures elite sub-accounts. — [Bitget support](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603844617)
- **TraderPro**: tournament finishers could win an elite-trader sub-account funded with 10,000 USDT and withdraw the profit share it earned. Bitget gives traders the starting capital. — [Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/bitget-to-award-elite-trader-subaccounts-worth-10000-usdt/) (year not stated in the snippet)
- **Cold-start promotion (2026-02-01 to 2026-03-31)**: open only to verified elite traders with **fewer than 5 copiers**. A CFD copy promotion (2026-04-21 to 2026-05-15) ranked elites by their own volume plus copier volume, and the top 336 shared 25,022 USDT. Copiers got loss protection on their first copy trade and up to 1,888 USDT in rewards. — [Bitget support](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603882773); [Bitget support](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603887459)
- Bitget also launched an upgraded spot elite system with "new rewards and enhanced exposure features", treating visibility as a reward. It launched **bot copy trading** in January 2026. — [TradingView/Chainwire](https://my.tradingview.com/news/chainwire:103f41913094b:0-bitget-launches-upgraded-spot-elite-trading-system-with-new-rewards-and-enhanced-exposure-features); [Bitget blog](https://www.bitget.cloud/blog/articles/bitget-bot-copy-trading-launch)

**Bybit Master Traders (press releases, 2024–2026)**
- WSOT Copy Trading Fest (October 2024): 100,000 USDT pool. — [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/bybits-wsot-copy-trading-fest-offers-100-000-usdt-prize-pool-for-crypto-investors-302285567.html)
- "Copy Trading Face-Off: human vs bot" (late 2024 to January 2025): 200,000 USDT, including prizes for "lucky voters". — [Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/human-vs-bot-bybit-announces-copy-trading-competition-with-200000-usdt-in-prizes-3745854)
- "Crypto Surf: copy traders and bots battle" (2025-04-18 to 2025-05-19): up to $250K. — [PR Newswire UK](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/bybit-launches-crypto-surf-copy-traders-and-bots-battle-for-250k-usdt-302432444.html)
- Master Trader Arena (November 2025): 300,000 USDT across two rounds of 150K. Teams are ranked by PnL. **50% of each team's prize goes to the Master Trader and the rest is split among followers pro rata by volume.** — [AAP/Cision](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20251117ae25988)
- View Master League (November–December 2025): a small 1,000 USDT airdrop with a "View Masters" title and 100 USDT each for the top 10. Status and title rewards cost very little. — [AAP/Cision](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20251114ae24847)
- "Master Trader's Showtime: TradFi vs Crypto" (April 2026): 200,000 USDT, top 50 per round, and each leader needs **at least 20 active followers**, which pushes leaders to recruit their own audiences. — [AAP/Cision](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20260429ae46583/)
- Boosted Trader Program: 180,000 USDT in copy-trading bonuses (date unclear). — [Traders Union](https://tradersunion.com/news/bonuses-and-promotions/show/1156899-bybit-review-boosted/)

**eToro Popular Investor (eToro pages)**
- Four tiers: Cadet, Champion, Elite, Elite Pro. Pay is based on average monthly equity and **average assets under copy (AUC)**, paid by the 10th of each month. Elite Pro earns 1.5% of annual AUC. The higher rate in a tier requires month-over-month AUC growth above 1.5%. — [eToro PI upgrade & payment guide](https://etoro.com/news-and-analysis/etoro-updates/pi-upgrade-and-payment-guide)
- An older eToro post listed flat stipends ($500/month at Rising Star, $1,000 at Champion). These are likely outdated. Third-party figures of 2–2.5% of AUC conflict with eToro's own 1.5%. — [eToro blog](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/etoro-updates/think-youre-a-great-trader-get-copied-get-paid-its-that-simple); [Bitcoinist](https://bitcoinist.com/how-does-etoro-popular-investor-program-work/amp/)
- eToro publishes coaching content for creators, such as "6 tips for growing your AUC". — [eToro](https://etoro.com/news-and-analysis/etoro-updates/6-tips-for-growing-your-auc-on-etoro)

**Hyperliquid vault leaders (official docs)**
- By default the vault leader receives 10% of vault profits. Leaders must keep their own capital in the vault: third parties say at least 5%, which I could not confirm in the official docs text. — [Hyperliquid docs: vaults](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/vaults); [OneKey](https://onekey.so/blog/ecosystem/best-hyperliquid-vault-framework/)

**Strategy marketplaces (Cryptohopper / 3Commas)**
- Cryptohopper keeps 30% of each marketplace sale and cuts that to 15% for sellers who list exclusively on Cryptohopper. Exclusivity applicants reportedly need an active community and a track record. Cryptohopper also runs a multi-level affiliate program. — [Cryptohopper docs: exclusivity](https://docs.cryptohopper.com/docs/sellers/exclusivity-program); [Cryptohopper affiliate blog](https://www.cryptohopper.com/blog/how-does-the-cryptohopper-multi-level-affiliate-program-work-7533)
- 3Commas: no official seller revenue-share terms found. A low-quality source says strategies are promoted by traders who earn referral commissions, which creates a "popularity over performance" conflict. — [usethisai](https://usethisai.pages.dev/crypto-trader/3commas) (low reliability)

**Fomo (consumer social trading app, 2025–2026)**
- Fomo runs "Trader Rewards" for users who spread trading ideas, plus "Clans" team trading. Many KOLs share Fomo referral codes. — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5212823)

### Inferences
- A 10% profit share is the industry standard. Tribe's "creators earn a share of Flash fees" is closer to Binance's extra **10% fee commission** and works even when the creator is losing money. That is a selling point to creators, and a conflict-of-interest risk to disclose to followers.
- The cheapest cold-start levers for a small team: (1) promotions limited to new leaders with fewer than 5 copiers, (2) prizes split 50/50 between leader and followers so leaders bring in followers, (3) title and badge rewards ("View Master"), (4) a minimum follower count to qualify, (5) a small grant of starting capital to the best trader in a tournament.
- eToro's model of paying for AUM growth suggests a Tribe variant: a bonus tied to copied-AUM growth, funded by Flash fee share.

### Gaps
- No reliable sources found on how Telegram bots, OKX or Bybit recruited specific early KOLs (fees paid, deal terms). Exchanges do not publish KOL deal terms.
- No official count of creators or followers for Bitget elite traders. Third-party counts conflict: 110K–190K elites and 520K–1M+ copiers ([Coin Bureau, Feb 2026](https://www.coinbureau.com/review/bitget-copy-trading-review)).

## Follower acquisition: competitions, leaderboards, referrals, points, PnL sharing, X/Farcaster, Base and partner programs

### Takeaway
The highest-leverage tactics with documented results are **points programs tied to trading plus referrals** (Hyperliquid), **referral fee shares of 25–30%** (Fomo, Maestro, BONKbot) and **seasonal prize-pool tournaments**. Distribution through paid social posting on X (InfoFi/Kaito) was **shut down by X in January 2026**, and Base's own creator-reward and content-coin bets were wound down in Q1 2026. Base now prioritizes "trading, payments, agents", which suits Tribe.

### Cited Findings
**Referral programs**
- Telegram bots (April 2024 survey): referral payouts range from 10% to 60% of fees depending on the bot. BONKbot paid 30% of referred users' fees in month 1, 20% in month 2 and 10% forever after. Photon used points on referral volume instead of fee share. — [Medium, 0xkryptokeisarii, Apr 2024](https://medium.com/@0xkryptokeisarii/all-telegram-trading-bot-referral-systems-listed-april-2024-cd043095e2b4)
- Maestro: reportedly a 3-tier referral program paying up to 25%. Banana Gun: reportedly 10% of referred users' fees. Both come from third-party reviews. — [DataWallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/best-crypto-telegram-bots); [CoinGecko](https://coingecko.com/learn/top-telegram-trading-bots)
- Banana Gun also buys Telegram Ads, advertising on the same platform its product runs on. — [telegramads.it, 2026](https://telegramads.it/blog/banana-gun-telegram-ads-profile-2026)
- Fomo: referrers get 25% of referred users' trading fees, and cumulative referral payouts passed $2M (reported mid-2026). — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5212823); [KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/fomo-surpasses-pump-fun-with-1-76m-daily-revenue)

**Points and airdrops**
- Hyperliquid: 1M points per week from 2023-11-01 to 2024-05-01 based on trading **and referral volume**, then 700K per week from 2024-05-29 to November 2024. Referrers earned 1 point for every 4 points their referees earned. 22K unique users received points in one weekly distribution (Privy case study). — [Hyperliquid docs: points](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/Hyperliquid-docs/points); [Privy case study](https://privy.io/blog/hyperliquid-case-study)
- The genesis airdrop on 2024-11-29 gave 31% of HYPE supply to about 90K–94K recipients (counts vary by source). One analysis says that after points ended, daily active traders rose about 8x, open interest about 3x and monthly volume about 6x. — [eco.com](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15039718-hyperliquid-airdrop-what-happened-and-what-s-next); [Myshterious Substack](https://myshterious.substack.com/p/hyperliquid-is-still-sticky-after)
- Fuul case study: HyperSignals grew perp volume 567% by running four incentive programs at once (vendor-reported). — [Fuul](https://www.fuul.xyz/case-studies/how-hypersignals-grew-perp-trading-volume-by-567-with-four-programs-at-once)
- Virtuals Genesis (launched 2025-04-17): users earn "Virgen Points" mainly by holding or staking, with less credit for trading, and pledge them for presale allocation (capped at 0.5% of supply per person). Active wallets went from 45 to 6,680 in one month and about $13M in volume (DappRadar). More than 8,300 unique addresses by early May 2025 (DWF). — [DappRadar](https://dappradar.com/blog/virtuals-genesis-revives-virtuals-protocol-with-10x-mega-boom); [DWF Labs](https://www.dwf-labs.com/research/552-virtuals-protocol-genesis-mechanism-analysis)

**Competitions**
- See the Bybit entries above (100K–300K USDT pools, 2024–2026), including formats where copy traders compete against bots. Binance's 20K USDC mock-portfolio campaign (April–May 2025) is a low-budget example.

**Distribution on X and Farcaster**
- Bankr: users tag the bot on Farcaster, or post or DM on X, and it parses commands in natural language. It launched on Farcaster and later expanded to X. — [Bankless, "defi by DMs"](https://www.bankless.com/defi-by-dms); [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/crypto/blogs/bankrbot-bankrcoin-onchain-agents-c06bff03-0c00-48c9-a45b-69b009a46c56/)
- **What backfired**: in mid-January 2026 X revoked API access for apps that pay users to post ("InfoFi"), citing AI slop and reply spam. Kaito sunset Yaps and moved to Kaito Studio, a creator-marketing product. Cookie DAO shut down Snaps. KAITO fell about 15–20%. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash); [Cointelegraph via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:15b1d1de4094b:0-kaito-winds-down-crypto-backed-yaps-as-x-bans-payments-for-ai-slop/); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/x-kills-kaito-infofi-tokens-crash)

**Base ecosystem programs**
- Base Builder Grants: retroactive, typically 1–5 ETH, more than 20 cohorts since March 2024. Weekly Builder Rewards (via Talent Protocol) pay up to 2 ETH per week and require Builder Score of at least 40 plus a human checkmark. A secondary report says the Talent Base campaign has ended. — [Base docs: get funded](https://docs.base.org/get-started/get-funded); [Talent docs](https://docs.talentprotocol.com/docs/legal/builder-rewards-terms-conditions)
- Base App Creator Rewards paid more than $450K to over 17,000 creators (about $26 each) over roughly 6 months and closed 2026-02-15. Zora content coins peaked in August 2025 (1.6M coins, about 3M traders, $470M volume), then crashed about 95%. On 2026-07-13 Brian Armstrong said content coins "didn't work" and Base's focus is "trading, payments, and agents (in that order)". **Base Creator Grants launched 2026-09-02**: up to $4,000 cash for creators making Base content, plus a $500 "Creator of the Week" track and amplification by regional accounts. The Base Ecosystem Fund has backed 30+ teams. — [eGamers](https://egamers.io/?p=105823)

### Inferences
- For Tribe, a copy-trade referral of about 25% of Flash fees, with a BONKbot-style declining schedule (30/20/10), is in line with the market and self-funding. A points season that credits both trading and referrals (Hyperliquid's 1-for-4 referral credit) is the best-documented cold-start mechanic. It carries airdrop expectations and attracts farmers.
- Do not build distribution on paid X posting. Organic PnL and copy cards, Farcaster/Base App frames, and the tag-a-bot model (Bankr) still work. Base Creator Grants ($4K) and Builder Grants (1–5 ETH) are realistic non-dilutive sources and amplification channels. Base's stated "trading + agents" focus helps Tribe's pitch.

### Gaps
- No published conversion data for PnL-card sharing (Fomo, Photon and others). I found no source quantifying it.
- No source found for Chainlink BUILD/SCALE or Definitive partner co-marketing programs relevant to small apps. This needs a direct check with those teams.
- Onchain Summer 2024/2025 results for trading apps were not researched within the tool budget.

## Positioning and messaging: what worked and what backfired

### Takeaway
"Copy the pros" and "get copied, get paid" (eToro) and "human vs bot" (Bybit) are common framings. Regulators in 2025–2026 are actively targeting finfluencers who promote "easy profit" copy trading, so safety and transparency framing (high-water marks, loss caps, disclosed fees) is both a differentiator and a compliance need. Tribe's Chainlink CRE guard supports this.

### Cited Findings
- eToro creator pitch: "Think You're a Great Trader? Get Copied, get paid: It's that Simple." — [eToro](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/etoro-updates/think-youre-a-great-trader-get-copied-get-paid-its-that-simple)
- Bybit pits copy traders against bots in its framing ("human vs bot", "copy traders and bots battle"), which turns choosing between a person and a strategy into a game. — [Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/human-vs-bot-bybit-announces-copy-trading-competition-with-200000-usdt-in-prizes-3745854); [PR Newswire UK](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/bybit-launches-crypto-surf-copy-traders-and-bots-battle-for-250k-usdt-302432444.html)
- Bitget offered copy-trade **loss protection** on a first trade (2026) to reduce the risk of trying it. — [Bitget support](https://www.bitget.com/support/articles/12560603887459)
- Regulatory backlash: in June 2025 ASIC issued warning notices to 18 finfluencers in a joint action with 8 other regulators, including those who invite people to "copy their trades". The NZ FMA lists copy trading as a priority risk (April 2026). An ASIC review followed an incident in which about 1,200 young Australians were wiped out within 48 hours when a "leader" went rogue. ESMA issued supervisory expectations for copy trading under MiFID II in March 2023. — [SMS Magazine](https://smsmagazine.com.au/news/2025/06/12/asic-moves-against-18-finfluencers/); [FMA](https://www.fma.govt.nz/news/all-releases/media-releases/tackling-unlawful-influencers/); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/aussie-regulator-joins-crackdown-against-finfluencers-issues-18-notices/); [PwC Legal](https://legal.pwc.de/en/news/articles/esma-communicates-supervisory-expectations-on-copy-trading-services)
- Strategy-marketplace referral incentives can reward popularity over performance (low-quality source). — [usethisai](https://usethisai.pages.dev/crypto-trader/3commas)
- Fomo founders: consumer crypto apps failed because they targeted a tiny DAU base (50–100K), and "the product itself is the only marketing". Erlanger: "Once we added Apple Pay, users flooded in." — [Alea Research podcast digest](https://alearesearch.io/podcast-digest/empire/fomo-co-founders-on-building-the-social-network-for-finance)
- Base and Zora's "every post is a coin" positioning collapsed: content coins fell about 95%, and leadership said it "didn't work". — [eGamers](https://egamers.io/?p=105823)

### Inferences
- Lead with "copy with guardrails" (CRE risk guard, max drawdown, no profit share without new highs) and "creators earn when you trade". Avoid guaranteed-return wording, "AI trades for you, profit" claims, and payment for promotional posts. All three are regulatory or platform-ban risks as of 2025–2026.
- Removing onboarding friction (Fomo's Apple Pay) mattered more than campaigns. For Tribe, smart wallets and a fiat onramp on Base fill that role.

### Gaps
- No A/B or conversion data comparing messages ("copy the pros" vs "safe copy trading" vs "AI").

## Metrics: conversion, CAC, retention, creator ratio

### Takeaway
Hard benchmarks are scarce and almost all self-reported. Useful reference points: Bitget's 2023 copy-trade report, the elite-to-copier ratio (roughly 1:4 to 1:10), Fomo's growth curve, and Base's average creator-reward payout of about $26.

### Cited Findings
- Bitget H1 2023 report (self-reported): 109K+ followers profitable, 74M USDT in cumulative profit, 93% of futures copiers and 82% of spot copiers "made profits" (not net returns). 44% of copy traders are under 25. Western Europe 31%, East Asia 26%, SE Asia 19%. Over 70% of profitable futures copiers follow more than 7 elite traders on average. — [Bitget blog](https://www.bitget.com/blog/articles/bitget-report-44-percent-of-copy-traders-are-gen-z); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/gen-z-dominates-crypto-copy-trading-space-85-make-profit/)
- Bitget creator-to-follower ratio (third-party, conflicting): 190K+ elites and 800K+ copiers (Coin Bureau, Feb 2026), about 1:4. An older BCG-cited figure was about 55K traders to 1.1M followers, about 1:20. — [Coin Bureau](https://www.coinbureau.com/review/bitget-copy-trading-review); [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/tag/bitget-ftx)
- Fomo: May 2025 beta with 35K traders, user growth of about 10% per week. November 2025: 120K+ users and about $700M volume, $17M Series A (Benchmark). June 2026: 600K+ (Tangem says 625K) users, $4B+ volume, $75M Series B at a $550M valuation (Index, USV). Volume went from $500M in June to $1.5B in July (CryptoRank). Conflicting figures: Wikipedia lists year-end-2025 volume as $20–40M, and one Bloomingbit headline claims 2.7M users. — [TradeInformer](https://www.tradeinformer.com/broker-news/fomo-raises-75-million-series-b-led-by-index-ventures); [Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/news/investments/28926-fomo-raises-75m-series-b-surpasses-625k-users/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fomo_(platform)); [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/deals/mobula-m-a-2026-08-31)
- Base App Creator Rewards averaged about $26 per creator (more than $450K across 17,000+ creators), a small incentive per creator. — [eGamers](https://egamers.io/?p=105823)
- Bankr: 1.2% share of Base launchpads in January 2025, 74% by May 2026, and $4.35B cumulative volume. One analyst puts it at about 1–3% the size of a major trading bot. — [Block Media](https://www.blockmedia.co.kr/research-english/1108445); [Crypto AI Tracker](https://crypto-ai-tracker.vercel.app/projects/bankr)

### Inferences
- When planning, assume a small creator base relative to followers (roughly 1 creator per 4–20 copiers on exchanges). Copiers spread across many leaders (more than 7 per profitable Bitget copier), so Tribe should make "copy a basket of creators" easy.

### Gaps
- No credible public CAC benchmarks for crypto copy-trading or bot users were found.
- No copier retention cohorts (D30/D90) published by any platform. Bitget shows leaders their retention but does not publish it.
- No reliable data on the percentage of users who become creators.

## Examples of small or new crypto apps that launched social-trading or bot features (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Recent winners combined (1) distribution inside an existing social surface (Telegram, X/Farcaster), (2) fee-share referrals, (3) an incentive season (points or token launches), and (4) extremely low onboarding friction. Several also show how quickly hype fades (Zora, Kaito, Virtuals agent tokens).

### Cited Findings
- **Fomo** (2025–2026): leaderboards, trade feeds, copy trading, Clans, Trader Rewards, 25% referral fee share and Apple Pay onboarding. Metrics are in the section above. — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5212823); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/fomo-raise-series-b-trading-app-550m-valuation)
- **Bankr** (2024–2026): an AI agent you tag on Farcaster or X to trade and launch tokens on Base. BNKR daily volume rose about 5x after the Coinbase listing in late July 2025 ($33M on listing day). The token is down about 65% from its February 2026 high. Fees are reported at more than $3.7M. — [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/the-bankr-boom); [Alea Research](https://alearesearch.substack.com/p/bankr-ai-execution-layer); [Crypto AI Tracker](https://crypto-ai-tracker.vercel.app/projects/bankr)
- **Virtuals Genesis** (April 2025): a points-gated agent launchpad. Active wallets went from 45 to 6,680 in one month, VIRTUAL doubled in late April 2025, and individual launches spiked (Basis OS about 40x in 12 days to about $5.5M market cap). There is no long-term performance data. — [DappRadar](https://dappradar.com/blog/virtuals-genesis-revives-virtuals-protocol-with-10x-mega-boom); [cnyes/BlockBeats](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/5954471)
- **Hyperliquid** (2023–2024): points from trading and referrals led to the biggest airdrop of the cycle and kept growing after points ended. See the follower-acquisition section above.
- **Telegram bots** (BONKbot, Maestro, Banana Gun; 2023–2024): tiered or declining referral fee shares plus Telegram Ads. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@0xkryptokeisarii/all-telegram-trading-bot-referral-systems-listed-april-2024-cd043095e2b4)
- **Kaito-style mindshare campaigns** (2024 to January 2026): effective for awareness until X banned paid posting apps in January 2026. Yaps also drew backlash over spam and smaller-than-expected allocations. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/354736/elon-musks-x-bans-access-infofi-crypto-projects-ai-slop-backlash)

### Inferences
- A realistic hackathon-budget playbook based on these cases: invite-only creator cohort plus a cold-start promotion for new leaders, a 4–8 week "Season 1" leaderboard with a small USDC pool split 50/50 between creator and copiers, a referral fee share from Flash fees, shareable PnL and copy cards, a tag-to-copy bot on Farcaster or X (Bankr model), and Base Builder or Creator grants for funding and amplification. Avoid pay-to-post and promising returns.

### Gaps
- Moby: no data found within the tool budget.
- No public postmortems of failed small copy-trading launches.
